## Quick glossary

- **Venue:** An operating location inside a business; access may be scoped to a venue
- **FOH / BOH:** Front-of-house / back-of-house teams, with delegated management boundaries
- **RLS:** PostgreSQL row-level security, one of the server-side isolation controls
- **Synthetic showcase:** Isolated, fictional test data used for internal evaluation only
- **Accepted feature:** A capability with the required evidence of implemented and tested behavior; a backlog entry alone is not acceptance
