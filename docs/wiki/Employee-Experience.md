## Joining a workplace

An employee receives an invitation from an authorized manager and completes the account flow. Their session and memberships determine which venue/department information they can access. Invitation delivery depends on configured account-service email; it is not equivalent to general-purpose messaging.

## Typical shift week

1. Open **My schedule** (`/my-schedule`) and inspect published upcoming shifts and the selected weekly schedule.
2. Open **Availability** (`/availability`) to maintain supported recurring patterns, date blocks and leave or availability exceptions.
3. For an offered vacancy, **Claim shift** or decline; interest does not automatically fill the shift. Wait for the manager's confirmed assignment.
4. Where applicable, request a shift swap and follow each worker-consent and management-approval step.
5. Open **Clock in / out** (`/attendance`) to record actual shifts and paid or unpaid breaks. Verify the recorded outcome.
6. Inspect personal attendance history and submit a correction request when a record is missing or wrong. A request does not silently overwrite the source clock event.
7. Read in-app notifications and announcements. Acknowledgements and read state follow the relevant workflow.

## Mobile and accessibility

The guide includes a narrow-screen employee walkthrough. Supported interactions should be usable by keyboard and assistive technology as well as touch, but the current browser regression has known failures under [issue #253](https://github.com/farbotkol/butterbox/issues/253); do not claim universal WCAG acceptance based on a single focused green run.

## Privacy and employment records

Employees should see their own supported attendance and schedule records, not another worker's profile or management-only payroll exports. The authoritative attendance record and correction/audit trail must be preserved, and ambiguous submissions should be checked before retrying.
