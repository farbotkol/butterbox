# Feature status, boundaries and limitations

> **Important:** This wiki reflects repository documentation, not a release certification. Always check the [User Guide](../USER_GUIDE.md), [progress evidence](../../IMPLEMENTATION_PROGRESS.md) and [product backlog](../../BUTTERBOX_PRODUCT_BACKLOG.md) for the status of a particular feature.

## Implemented vs planned

The [User Guide](../USER_GUIDE.md) describes behavior documented as available in the maintained roadmap. It explicitly warns that role, venue, department and environment can affect availability. The [features not yet generally available or accepted](../USER_GUIDE.md#features-not-yet-generally-available-or-accepted) section identifies important omissions.

The [product backlog](../../BUTTERBOX_PRODUCT_BACKLOG.md) describes planned work. An issue or design in that backlog is **not** proof of implementation, full acceptance, security validation or production readiness.

## Synthetic showcase

The [internal synthetic showcase](../USER_GUIDE.md#internal-synthetic-showcase) is a separately prepared, isolated demonstration using fictional data. It is for internal evaluation, not a live customer environment. Provisioned access and setup restrictions are documented there and in the [operator README](../../README.md#isolated-local-synthetic-showcase).

## Payroll and reporting

The [timesheet and payroll-preparation guide](../USER_GUIDE.md#timesheets-and-payroll-preparation) and [generic payroll CSV notes](../../README.md#generic-payroll-csv) describe supported preparation/export behavior. Do not imply that a generic export automates external provider processing or guarantees compliance with all Australian awards.

## Product decisions and terminology

Consult the [decision log](../DECISION_LOG.md) before claiming a feature or policy is approved. Read [AGENTS.md](../../AGENTS.md) for development guardrails and [Home](Home.md#quick-glossary) for this wiki's short glossary.

If source documents conflict, preserve the discrepancy and resolve it through the canonical owner/issue review process rather than silently choosing a more convenient claim.
